ATLAS

3D product configurator with real-time material swaps and camera-guided storytelling.

ROLESolo developer
TIMELINE7 weeks
STACKThree.js / React / GLSL

The Problem

Product pages with static photos can't show what a customer actually gets when they change a color or material — every option needs a new photoshoot, and customers are left guessing.

The Approach

  • Built a real-time 3D configurator in Three.js so material and color swaps render instantly on the actual model, no new photography needed.
  • Wrote custom GLSL shaders for accurate material rendering — brushed metal, fabric, and matte finishes each needed different lighting math to look right.
  • Choreographed camera movement to guide users through key views automatically, instead of leaving them to figure out orbit controls.

Key Decisions

Getting realistic materials in-browser without tanking frame rate meant baking as much lighting as possible ahead of time and keeping the real-time shader work limited to what actually needed to react to user input — color and surface finish, not shadows or reflections.
